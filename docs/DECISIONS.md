| ADR-027 | Refund requests are eligible only for UNOPENED products within 30 days of payment (orders.paid_at + 30 days; 409 REFUND_WINDOW_CLOSED after). Return shipping logistics (who pays, labels, receiving, inspection) are out of scope. Supersedes ADR-023 "no returns" for this case. | Keep ADR-023 (no returns, keep or dispose); any-condition refunds with no window; a public bearer review link with no login; restock returned unopened stock | Matches a common supplement return policy and limits refund exposure to recent, unopened orders; the system cannot verify "unopened", so it is an attested fact the provider weighs; requiring the owning provider on the link keeps money movement behind auth; writing off every shipped return keeps ADR-013 simple and safe for ingestibles (no re-sale of goods that left Cerbo's control). | Approved |
| ADR-028 | Providers price each order line with a per-unit DOLLAR MARKUP (`markupCents`, integer cents, required, 0..100000 = $0..$1,000); the patient pays COGS + markup + the 0.75% fee. The markup is stored per line (`order_lines.unit_markup_cents`) and per schedule line (`schedule_lines.unit_markup_cents`) for tracking and audit. A draft re-derives its price from the CURRENT COGS and is re-priced from the snapshot COGS at send; each recurring cycle prices from current COGS + the schedule's markup. Cerbo Admin no longer sets or sees a list price or margin (list-price editor, margin-at-list and the profit simulator are removed); admin sees markup only in order audit and metrics. Supersedes ADR-005 (margin %), ADR-025 (admin list price) and the price clause of ADR-024; amends ADR-002 (the fee is now built into the patient's price; the patient still sees no fee line). Rounding rule (round once per order; amends ADR-001 and ADR-003): need = sum(qty x (unitCogs + markup)); the patient total is rounded ONCE, subtotal = ceil(need x 10000 / (10000 - feeBps)); the platform fee is 0.75% of the subtotal rounded UP to the cent, once per order, orderFee = ceil(subtotal x feeBps / 10000) (never below 0.75%, at most 1 cent above). Because both are rounded up, subtotal - need = fee exactly: nothing is left over as rounding, and the provider nets EXACTLY qty x markup on every line (a $0 markup nets exactly $0.00). The subtotal is split across lines by largest remainder of each line's exact share, so line totals add up to the subtotal to the cent; line fee = line total - line cogs - qty x markup, each the line's exact 0.75% rounded down or up (within 1 cent), adding up to the order fee. Line fees are stored (`order_lines.line_fee_cents`), used by refunds (ADR-012 unchanged) and shown in the audit, but not on the provider order builder (the order fee is shown once in the Order summary). A line whose total is not divisible by its qty shows an average unit price (half-up, labelled "avg"); the line total is what is charged. The audit names every cent: subtotal = COGS + fee + sum(qty x markup), checked on every order (`order.every_cent_accounted`, and `order.price_matches_markup` re-prices the order from the stored snapshot COGS and markups). Orders with any line priced before ADR-028 (no markup) keep their stored prices and the price-based split(). | Keep margin % (price = COGS / (1 - margin)); dollar markup with the provider absorbing the fee; keep an admin list price as the default | A dollar amount is what providers think in and needs no percentage maths; grossing up means the provider keeps exactly the markup they typed on every line, so no markup-priced line can go negative (the ADR-004 floor always holds; MARGIN_NEGATIVE can only come from legacy pre-ADR-028 lines), and a COGS rise passes through to the patient instead of putting a recurring cycle on hold | Approved |

## Open business risks (for the final write-up)
- Card processing typically costs well above 0.75%. If Cerbo absorbs processing fees, each order loses money. Pricing model must account for it.
- Post-fulfillment refunds cost Cerbo full COGS while the provider loses only the fee. ADR-027 narrows patient requests to unopened products within 30 days of payment; shipped returns are still written off.
- Migration measurement depends on a trustworthy baseline source.
