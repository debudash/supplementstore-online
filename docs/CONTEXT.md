# CONTEXT.md - Business and product context

## Company
Cerbo (cer.bo) is an all-in-one EHR for independent practices: functional and integrative medicine, direct primary care, cash-based practices, IV and BHRT clinics, and medical spas. Its existing product already includes in-house inventory management for supplements, recurring billing and subscriptions, a patient portal where patients pay invoices, and integrations with online supplement storefronts.

## Users
- **Provider** - a clinician who recommends supplements as part of a patient's treatment plan. In this project a provider belongs to a practice (1:1 in seed data).
- **Patient** - receives a payment link, pays for the order, and may authorize recurring orders.
- **Cerbo Admin** - Cerbo staff. Owns inventory, fulfillment, refunds oversight, and the business metrics.

## Problem today
Providers order supplements on third-party marketplaces on the patient's behalf. The provider pays up front, the product ships to the patient, and the patient reimburses the provider separately. As a result:
- Providers front money and chase reimbursement
- The patient experience is disconnected from care
- Nothing appears in the clinical record or practice financials
- All transaction value, margin, and fees flow through systems Cerbo does not own

## What we are building
A working vertical slice where:
1. A provider assembles a supplement order for a patient and sets the patient-facing price
2. The patient pays Cerbo directly (stubbed payment)
3. The system computes and records the split: item cost (COGS, kept by Cerbo since Cerbo holds inventory), provider margin (owed to the provider), and a 75 bps platform fee (Cerbo revenue)
4. Every cent of a paid or refunded order is traceable

Plus: refunds, recurring orders, provider sales and payable reporting, Cerbo-owned inventory with stock checks and reservations, and a Cerbo Admin report that measures GMV, fee revenue, and migration off third-party marketplaces against a baseline.

## Business goal and metrics
- **Headline metric:** GMV processed in-house and the 75 bps earned on it
- **Leading indicator:** volume migrated off third-party marketplaces
- Exact metric definitions are in `docs/METRICS.md`

## Constraints
- US only, USD only
- Regulatory, compliance, tax, and shipping cost are out of scope
- Payments, auth, email, shipping, and payouts are stubbed behind clean interfaces
- Deployed to Railway for the demo

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| GMV | Gross merchandise value: total amount patients paid |
| COGS | Cerbo's unit cost of a SKU, snapshotted onto the order when it is sent |
| Platform fee | 0.75% (75 bps) of the order subtotal, rounded up to the cent once per order (ADR-028), built into the patient's price |
| Provider margin | Order total - COGS - platform fee. Owed to the provider (payee is the practice) |
| Settled GMV | Total GMV - refunded GMV |
| Sunk fee | The platform fee Cerbo keeps on a refunded order |
| Snapshot | Price and COGS copied onto order lines at send time. Never recomputed from the live catalog |
| Journal | One balanced group of ledger entries for one money event (payment, refund) |
| Reservation | Stock held for a sent, unpaid order. Expires after 7 days |
| Baseline | A provider's historical external marketplace supplement GMV, used to measure migration |
| Synthetic | Demo data generated by Cerbo Admin. Flagged and excludable from every metric |
